Summary

David Guevel is a machine learning engineer working on perception and embodied AI for autonomous vehicles, with a research background in high-energy astrophysics. He earned a PhD in Physics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where he searched for the sources of astrophysical neutrinos with the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and studied TeV gamma-ray sources with the Swift X-ray telescope, followed by a postdoctoral appointment applying deep learning to gamma-ray telescope data. He now applies that same rigor in statistics, Monte Carlo methods, and maximum likelihood analysis to modern computer vision and robotics problems in the automotive industry, building 3D scene reconstruction, vision-language-action models, and real-time mapping systems at Volkswagen and General Motors.
